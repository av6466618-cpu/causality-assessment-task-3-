  <h2>1. What is Causality Assessment?</h2>
  <p>
    Causality assessment (also known as relatedness assessment) determines the causal relationship or association between exposure to a drug/medicine and an adverse event [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=8s">00:00:08</a>].
    Currently, this task is primarily carried out by medical reviewers or safety physicians within pharmaceutical companies [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=83s">00:01:23</a>].
  </p>

  <h2>2. Key Factors Influencing Causality</h2>
  <p>Four major factors are evaluated when determining causality [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=116s">00:01:56</a>]:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Drug-related:</strong> Known profile, labeling, concomitant medications, predisposing factors [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=126s">00:02:06</a>].</li>
    <li><strong>Event-related:</strong> Nature and characteristics of the adverse event [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=155s">00:02:35</a>].</li>
    <li><strong>Patient-related:</strong> Medical history, past allergic history, pharmacogenomics/genetic predisposition [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=175s">00:02:55</a>].</li>
    <li><strong>Clinical factors:</strong> Temporal relationship (time-to-onset), de-challenge and re-challenge outcomes, response to corrective treatment [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=232s">00:03:52</a>].</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>3. Common Assessment Methods</h2>
 
  <h3>Naranjo Algorithm</h3>
  <p>
    Uses a questionnaire format containing 10 specific questions with answers scored as 'Yes', 'No', or 'Do Not Know' [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=352s">00:05:52</a>].
    The cumulative score categorizes causality into:
  </p>
  <table>
    <thead>
      <tr>
        <th>Total Score</th>
        <th>Causality Category</th>
      </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr>
        <td>&ge; 9</td>
        <td>Definite</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>5 – 8</td>
        <td>Probable</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>1 – 4</td>
        <td>Possible</td>
      </tr>
      <tr>
        <td>&le; 0</td>
        <td>Doubtful</td>
      </tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>

  <h3>WHO-UMC Causality Assessment System</h3>
  <p>
    Jointly developed by the World Health Organization and Uppsala Monitoring Centre [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=640s">00:10:40</a>].
    Categories include:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>Certain</li>
    <li>Probable / Likely</li>
    <li>Possible</li>
    <li>Unlikely</li>
    <li>Conditional / Unclassified</li>
    <li>Unassessable / Unclassifiable</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Special Concepts</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>Implied Causality:</strong> Spontaneous (unsolicited) reports submitted by healthcare professionals or consumers carry implied causality because effort was taken to report it [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=740s">00:12:20</a>].
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>AE vs. ADR:</strong> An Adverse Event (AE) has unknown/unassessed causality, whereas an Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) has an established or suspected causal relationship [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=824s">00:13:44</a>].
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Vaccine Causality (AEFI):</strong> Adverse Events Following Immunization (AEFI) utilize a specialized 4-step WHO algorithm [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=898s">00:14:58</a>] evaluating factors such as product quality, administration/anxiety process, and biological plausibility [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=994s">00:16:34</a>].
    </li>
  </ul>

  <h2>5. Future Outlook: Automation</h2>
  <p>
    Companies are exploring machine learning and artificial intelligence models trained on historic case data to automate parts of the causality assessment process [<a class="timestamp" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gH4AWnwTDI&t=1188s">00:19:48</a>].
  </p>
